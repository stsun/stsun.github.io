I am an oceanographer at the Laoshan Laboratory in Qingdao, China. Prior to this appointment, I was a postdoc at the California Institute of Technology and an Assistant Professor at Florida State University. I obtained my PhD from the Scripps Institution of Oceanography in 2019.

My group's research is rooted in ocean dynamics and seeks to advance fundamental understanding of the ocean's role in the climate system. We focus on the physical mechanisms governing ocean circulation and its response to climate change — from the global overturning circulation to regional currents — and their implications for heat and carbon transport. More broadly, I am interested in the interactions among the ocean, atmosphere, cryosphere, and marine ecosystems.
